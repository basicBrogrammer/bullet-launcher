<p><b>Your home screen is a bullet journal.</b></p>
<p>Bullet Launcher is a minimal Android launcher built around rapid logging. Open your phone to the Daily Log — capture tasks, events, and notes — then reach apps from a simple bottom dock.</p>
<p><br><b>BULLET JOURNAL HOME</b></p>
<ul>
<li><b>Monthly · Daily · Future</b> — swipe between logs on the home screen.</li>
<li><b>Rapid logging</b> — • tasks, ○ events, – notes, plus ★ priority.</li>
<li><b>Tap to complete</b> — long-press to edit or delete (never an accidental wipe).</li>
<li><b>FAB +</b> — add a bullet with type, schedule, and tags.</li>
<li><b>Index</b> — jump to Unscheduled and tag collections like Personal or Work.</li>
</ul>
<p><br><b>HOME APPS &amp; GESTURES</b></p>
<ul>
<li><b>Bottom dock</b> — up to 5×3 app slots; one slot opens the full app drawer.</li>
<li><b>Swipe up</b> — expands the home apps sheet (never opens the full drawer).</li>
<li><b>Drawer button</b> — opens the full app list as an overlay so the dock stays usable.</li>
<li><b>Swipe down</b> — closes the drawer or collapses the sheet; notifications/search only when both are closed.</li>
<li><b>Home</b> — closes the drawer and returns you to the Daily Log.</li>
</ul>
<p><br><b>CALENDAR SYNC</b></p>
<p>Event bullets can sync both ways with your device / Google Calendar. Choose which calendars appear under Settings → Calendars to sync.</p>
<p><br><b>PRIVACY &amp; PRINCIPLES</b></p>
<ul>
<li>No ads.</li>
<li>No account required.</li>
<li>Open source (GPLv3).</li>
<li>Journal data stays on your device; calendar and location are only used when you enable those features.</li>
</ul>
<p><br><b>GETTING STARTED</b></p>
<ol>
<li>Install Bullet Launcher.</li>
<li>Set it as your default Home app when Android asks.</li>
<li>Use the seeded sample bullets on first launch to learn the journal, then replace them with yours.</li>
</ol>
<p>Started from Olauncher — reshaped into a bullet-journal home screen while keeping the same spirit of simplicity.</p>
